# Verified newer-data leads

These are research leads identified for extending the dataset. They are not yet merged into `data/raw/master_dataset.csv` because the current repository expects locality/day rows and verified flood labels.

## IMD Chennai station data

IMD's Chennai city pages expose station-level extreme-weather tables. For August 2024, the Chennai-Meenambakkam station page reports 24-hour rainfall of 45.6 mm and monthly total 156.7 mm; Chennai-Nungambakkam reports 47.5 mm and 92.1 mm respectively. The same pages show that 2025/2026 August values are not yet populated in those historical extreme-event tables.

Source: https://city.imd.gov.in/citywx/extreme_data_view.php?id=43279
Source: https://city.imd.gov.in/citywx/extreme_data_view.php?id=43278

## IMD 2026 current rainfall

The IMD Regional Meteorological Centre, Chennai publishes districtwise daily/seasonal rainfall distributions. A 14-Aug-2026 report provides Chennai rainfall of 0.5 mm for the day and 228.6 mm cumulative for the Southwest Monsoon period from 01-Jun-2026 to 14-Aug-2026.

Source: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/chennai/mcdata/dailyweekly.pdf

## Important treatment

The station and district data above are valid newer weather observations, but they are not equivalent to the project's 30-locality historical rows. They should first be placed into a separate source-specific ingestion table, then mapped to the project localities only when a defensible spatial mapping is defined.

Do not append these aggregate station/district observations directly as if they were measurements for every locality. That would duplicate the same rainfall value across localities and artificially increase the training sample size.
